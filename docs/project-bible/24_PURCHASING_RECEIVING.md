## Purchasing and Receiving
Purchase is a document; receiving/inspection is physical. One bill can have many lines. Received/Accepted/Rejected are distinct. Shortage=Billed−Received; Accepted+Rejected=Received; only Accepted enters stock. Supplier claims, credits, disputes, replacement and returns link to original purchase/line. Attachments link to purchase. Controlled purchase→receipt→inspection→stock→payment tests passed historically.
