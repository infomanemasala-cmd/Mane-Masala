## Sub-Agents
Sub-Agent is the billing party; end-customer traceability is separate. End customer may be existing, named-unregistered or anonymous. LIVE orders retain order_party_type, sub_agent_id and end-customer fields while orders.customer_id remains billing owner. Late-September migrations and production branch changes strengthened Customer/Sub-Agent routing and filtering.
