## Orders and Reservations
Order creation starts planning. Planning checks current/reserved/available stock, reserves usable finished stock, calculates shortfall, selects recipe version, calculates production inputs and procurement needs, then presents wife approval. Statuses include Draft, Received, Production Planned, Confirmed, In Production, Ready, Partially Dispatched, Dispatched, Completed and appropriate Cancelled. One session may contain multiple orders; one order may have many lines. Reservations are commitments and must be consumed exactly once. A historical fix prevented partial approval from scaling a capped reservation twice.
