## Payments and Financials
Supplier and customer payments are separate. Methods include cash, UPI, bank transfer and other. Payments have actual dates, amounts, method and references/notes; allocations connect payments to invoices/purchases. Unallocated money is an explicit advance. Outstanding is view-derived, not a manually editable balance. D4 Round 3 validated key supplier-outstanding cases; discount/charges was not exercised because controlled values were zero.
